# 30-Day Spanish Comprehensible Input Plan

Oct 30, 2026 · @Henrik Aasen Kjeldsberg

## Overview

Keep Dreaming Spanish as the core and add one Latin American podcast for audio-only days. At 20-30 minutes a day, 30 days gives about 12-15 hours of input. That is a solid habit block, not a level jump: on the Dreaming Spanish roadmap, comfortable daily conversation sits around 1,000 hours and understanding normal-speed speech around 600.

- **Start point:** finishing A2, with grammar study and tutor practice behind you. In Dreaming Spanish terms, start at Intermediate (levels 3-4) and drop to Beginner only if you understand less than about 80%.
- **Goal for 30 days:** 25 of 30 days completed, 12+ hours logged, and Intermediate videos at normal speed feeling easy by week 4.
- **Main rule:** the input must be understood and interesting. If you are translating in your head, the video is too hard; if you are bored, switch topic, not method.
- **Bonus minutes count.** Podcasts on walks or while cooking are extra hours on top of the daily session, never a replacement for it.

## Resources

Nothing beats Dreaming Spanish for leveled video input, so it stays the core. The additions cover what it lacks: audio for screen-free time, transcripts for your current transcript-training phase, and real Canarian speech around you.

| Local input in Puerto de la Cruz | TV, radio, shops, signs | Canarian | Weekly real-world listening | Free |

Canarian Spanish is a good fit for a Latin American preference: like most of Latin America, it uses *ustedes* rather than *vosotros* and pronounces *z* and *c* as *s*. Premium Dreaming Spanish is worth it only for the hour tracker and full library; the free YouTube channel is enough to run this plan.

## Daily session

Three session types rotate through the week. Each fits 20-30 minutes with no setup beyond opening the app.

**A. Watch day (most days, 20-30 min)**

1. Pick 1-3 Dreaming Spanish Intermediate videos on topics you actually care about.
2. Watch without subtitles. Rewind once if a key sentence is lost; otherwise keep going.
3. Rate comprehension after each video: above 90% means try a harder level next time, below 75% means go easier.

**B. Transcript day (2 per week, 25 min)**

1. Listen to one podcast segment (8-10 min) with no text.
2. Listen again while reading the transcript.
3. Listen a third time without text. Note at most 5 phrases worth keeping; no flashcards needed.

**C. Real-world day (1 per week, 20-30 min)**

1. Watch local TV news or listen to Canarian radio, or sit in a café and listen.
2. Use the 5 phrases from the week in one real exchange: shop, bar or your tutor.

## 30-day schedule

Each week uses 4 watch days, 2 transcript days and 1 real-world day; the difficulty rises one notch per week. Days are numbered so the plan can start any day.

| Week | Days | Focus | Level | Target by end of week |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1-7 | Find your level and build the habit | DS Intermediate, slower guides; Duolingo Podcast for transcript days | 7 sessions done; a level where you understand 80-90% |
| 2 | 8-14 | Volume at a comfortable level | DS Intermediate, any guide; Españolistos for transcript days | About 3.5 hours total; no rewinding most videos |
| 3 | 15-21 | Faster speech and new accents | DS upper Intermediate, mixed countries; Cesar Intermediate | Normal-speed Intermediate videos feel comfortable |
| 4 | 22-28 | Stretch toward real speech | DS Advanced short videos twice; local TV or radio on the real-world day | Follow the gist of a 10-min Advanced video |
| End | 29-30 | Review | Rewatch two week-1 videos | Notice how much easier they are; set next month's level |

Missed a day: skip it, never double up. Two missed days in a row: next session is a 10-minute easy video, just to restart the habit.

## Tracking

Log minutes daily; review once a week in under 5 minutes.

- **Hours:** Dreaming Spanish's tracker logs videos automatically; add podcast and outside minutes manually there as "outside hours".
- **Weekly check:** comprehension level (rough %), what felt hard, one topic to look for next week.
- **Speaking:** keep tutor sessions as they are. Comprehensible input feeds them; it does not replace them.
- **Signal you are ready to level up:** three videos in a row above 90% without rewinding.
